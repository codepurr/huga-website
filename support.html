Subscription

To cancel or change your subscription, open Settings on your iPhone or iPad, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Huga. To restore a subscription on a new device, tap + in Huga, then restore.

Delete your account

In Huga, tap the backpack, then account, then delete account. Your places, photos and account are deleted. An active subscription is billed by Apple until you cancel it in Settings.

Keep a copy of your places

Tap the backpack, then account, then export. Huga makes a file with all your places that you can save or send.
