Huga privacy policy

1 October 2026

Who runs Huga

Huga is made by Jana Greilich, who is responsible for your data. Postal address on request. Questions about your data: greilichjana@gmail.com.

What Huga keeps

Huga keeps your places in your account: country, city, name, address, link, description, colour and one photo per place. They are stored on Google Firebase servers, so they follow you to a new phone. Only you can see your places and your account.

You sign in with an email and password, or with Apple or Google. Huga keeps your email address and an account id. Passwords are stored by Firebase in protected form and never seen by Huga. Apple lets you hide the address.

Huga collects usage statistics and crash reports through Google Analytics for Firebase and Crashlytics, tied to your account id: which screens open, which actions happen, your device model and system version, and what went wrong before a crash. For this Firebase keeps an installation id on your device. No advertising id, no tracking across other apps.

What stays on your phone

If you allow location access, Huga uses your position only on the phone: to show how far your saved places are and to open the nearest city first. Your position is never stored or sent anywhere.

Archive files you share are made on the phone and go only where you send them.

Who else handles your data

Huga uses these services, each only to run Huga: Google Firebase (sign-in, storage, statistics, crash reports), Google Sign-In, Sign in with Apple, Apple Maps (address and pin search), Apple In-App Purchase and Cloudflare (account deletion). They protect your data at least as well as this policy does. They may process it in the United States and other countries. How Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple through your Apple Account. Huga never sees your payment details. Huga only learns from Apple whether your subscription is active.

Huga does not sell your data, show ads or share data with advertisers.

Why

Huga stores your account and places to give you the service you signed up for. It collects statistics and crash reports to find bugs and improve the app.

How long

Your account, places and photos stay until you delete them. Google keeps statistics tied to your account id for at most 14 months and crash reports for 90 days.

You can delete your account in the menu. The request goes to a Huga server on Cloudflare, which deletes your places, your photos and your account. If deletion fails, the server keeps the account id in its error log for at most 7 days.

Your rights

You can see and change your places in the app, export them as a file and delete everything with your account. To ask what Huga holds about you, to correct it, or to object to statistics, write to greilichjana@gmail.com. You can also complain to a data protection authority.

Children

Huga is not made for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new date appears at the top of this page.
